On the bar: your "before vs after their tooltip" clue is the giveaway. TooltipLib has a "deferred mode" path that fires when another tooltip framework overrides [code]DoTooltip[/code]. AMMS / Backpack mods are likely culprits. In deferred mode we paint a background extension (vanilla opacity) below the foreign framework's content before our provider rows render — that extension is what you're seeing as the bar.

Confirm in one step: set [code]TooltipLib.debug = true[/code] before your provider registers, hover a clothing item, then grep [code]console.txt[/code] for [code]deferred_mode[/code]. If that line shows up, it's our deferred path — and I'll fix it properly in v1.2.0 (seam color matching to whatever framework appended above us).

CleanUI is innocent here — I audited 42.16 end-to-end and it has zero overrides on [code]ISToolTipInv[/code] / [code]ObjectTooltip[/code] / [code]DoTooltip[/code]. The bar repros even without CleanUI on, just with AMMS/Backpack present.
